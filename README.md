# THE ETERNAL VOW — Royal South Indian Kalyanam Website (Sai & Shreya)

An award-winning, royal South Indian editorial wedding invitation website designed for **Sai & Shreya**. Built with **React**, **Vite**, **Tailwind CSS**, and **Framer Motion**, this experience combines the grandeur of traditional Vedic rituals with dreamy royal heritage artwork.

---

## ✦ Dreamy Royal & South Indian Heritage Elements

- **Regal Peacocks (`RoyalPeacock`)**:
  - Ornate crowned peacocks with turquoise & royal blue plumage, golden eye-spots (*Ocelli*), perched gracefully atop invitation crests and story chapters.
- **Royal Caparisoned Elephants (`RoyalElephant`)**:
  - Auspicious temple elephants (*Gaja Raja*) with raised trunks in salute (*Abhaya Mudra*), adorned with golden *Nettipattam* headgear and holding fresh pink lotuses.
- **Auspicious Love Birds / Parrots (`AuspiciousParrots`)**:
  - Traditional pair of green and ruby-collared parrots (*Meenakshi Killi*) perched on blooming jasmine garland twigs.
- **Dreamy Firefly Glow Canvas (`DreamyGlowParticles`)**:
  - Soft ambient golden stardust particles and fireflies drifting gently up through the background.
- **Cascading Jasmine & Marigold Garlands (`HangingGarland`)**:
  - Pure white *Mallipoo / Mogra* strings with red rose beads and golden marigolds flanking the screen.
- **Sacred Kuthu Vilakku (`KuthuVilakku`)**:
  - Traditional 5-wick brass temple deepams wrapped with fresh floral garlands and flickering flames.
- **Brass Urli with Lotus (`LotusUrli`)**:
  - Water vessel filled with blooming pink lotuses (*Kamalam*) and floating jasmine florets.
- **South Indian Mango & Marigold Toranam (`SouthIndianToran`)**:
  - Traditional jute rope with fresh green mango leaves (*Mavilai*) and radiant marigolds.
- **Temple Gopuram Zari Border (`TempleZariBorder`)**:
  - Kanjeevaram sari gold zari triangle temple borders dividing key sections.

## ✦ Website Sections

1. **Auspicious Royal Invitation Gateway (`IntroInvitation.jsx`)**: Peacocks, saluting elephants, Kuthu Vilakku, Ganesha invocation, and "Open Auspicious Kalyanam" transition.
2. **Hero Experience (`Hero.jsx`)**: Grand editorial portrait of Sai & Shreya, royal palace arches, saluting elephants, love birds, and Muhurtham badge.
3. **Wedding Announcement (`Announcement.jsx`)**: Traditional Vedic shlokas and family lineage cards for both ancestral roots (*Varudu & Vadhu*).
4. **Our Story (`Story.jsx`)**: 3 romantic editorial chapters (*Kyoto Meeting*, *Adventures*, and *Udaipur Proposal*) with peacock and floral branch accents.
5. **Sacred Kalyanam Rites (`SacredRituals.jsx`)**:
   - Mangalasnanam & Pelli Koduku / Pelli Kooturu (మంగళస్నానం)
   - Snathakam & Kashi Yatra (స్నాతకం & కాశీ యాత్ర)
   - Jeelakarra Bellam — Sumuhurtham (జీలకర్ర బెల్లం)
   - Kanyadaanam & Mangalya Sutra Dharanam (మాంగళ్యధారణం)
   - Talambralu Extravaganza (తాలంబ్రాలు)
   - Saptapadi & Arundhati Darshanam (సప్తపది & అరుంధతీ దర్శనం)
6. **Saptapadi: Seven Sacred Vows (`Saptapadi.jsx`)**: Interactive walk through the 7 eternal vows taken around the holy agni (sacred fire) framed by royal peacocks and elephants.
7. **Wedding Celebrations (`Events.jsx`)**: Traditional timeline for **Haldi**, **Mehendi**, **Sangeet**, **Kalyanam (Muhurtham)**, and **Grand Reception** with dress codes and Google Calendar sync.
8. **Live Countdown (`Countdown.jsx`)**: Real-time ticker counting down to the exact Muhurtham (6:32 PM, Rohini Nakshatra, Mithuna Lagnam).
9. **Editorial Gallery (`Gallery.jsx`)**: High-res couple photo grid with full-screen lightbox, keyboard arrows, and mobile touch swipe.
10. **The Destination (`Venue.jsx`)**: The Oberoi Udaivilas & Jagmandir Island (Udaipur), maps, transit distance, and hospitality concierge.
11. **Ashirwad — Showers of Blessings (`BlessingsWall.jsx`)**: Interactive celebratory shower of **Akshintalu (sacred turmeric rice), jasmine buds, and pearls** with digital guestbook.
12. **Closing & Blessings (`Closing.jsx`)**: Vedic peace mantras, family compliments, and Return to Top navigation.
